Follow `AGENTS.md`. Input: $ARGUMENTS

Review the writing, not the substance:
- **Structure:** does the order of sections serve the argument? Is the verdict easy to find? Is anything in the wrong place?
- **Argument flow:** do claims lead to evidence lead to judgement? Where does a reader lose the thread?
- **Clarity:** vague sentences, undefined terms, jargon without explanation, overlong paragraphs.
- **Opening and close:** does the first paragraph tell a reader why to keep going? Does the end land?

Report as numbered points, each quoting the passage it's about and saying what's wrong with it and why. **Don't** offer replacement wording or a restructured outline. That's his job.
