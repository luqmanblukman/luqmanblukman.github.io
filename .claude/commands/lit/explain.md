Follow `AGENTS.md` (tutor mode). Input: $ARGUMENTS

1. Get the paper (see `.claude/agents/paper-critic.md`, "Getting the paper").
2. Explain the requested concept or section briefly and plainly: what it is, and the one idea needed to follow the paper's use of it. Quote or cite where it appears (§, Fig., Eq.).
3. Explain only what was asked. Don't evaluate the paper, don't point out weaknesses, and don't connect it to other sections he hasn't asked about.
4. Hand it back with one question that makes him apply it, e.g. "Given that, what does Table 2 actually measure?"
